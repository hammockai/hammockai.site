Privacy

If we wrote to you

This notice is for the businesses Hammock AI wrote to with a website offer. It explains, in plain words, which details of your business we use, what for, for how long, and how to ask us not to write again.

Who is writing

Hammock AI, a studio that builds simple websites for small businesses. A person reviews and sends each message, one at a time: no mass mailings and no programs that write on their own.

Responsible for this data: [name or legal name of the person or company responsible]. Contact: [correo de contacto del Capitán].

What data we use

Only what your business published so people can contact it. We take it by hand, from your own channel:

  • your business name, its trade and its area;
  • the public channel we wrote to: your email or the form on your website [or your Instagram account, if that channel is approved];
  • where and when we saw that detail, and what we noticed about your business online (for example, that it has no website).

We don't buy databases or use programs that copy data in bulk. We don't keep personal details about the owner beyond the business name, and no sensitive data, such as health data.

What for

To write to you once with a website proposal and, if you don't reply, send a single reminder. If you reply, to talk with you. Nothing else.

How long we keep it

  • If you don't reply, we delete your data [60 days, period to be confirmed] after the first message.
  • If you tell us no, we delete your data that same day. We only keep your domain or username on a "do not contact" list, so we never write to you again.
  • If we talk or work together, we keep what's needed while the conversation or the work lasts.

How to ask us not to write again

Reply "NO" to our message or write to [correo de contacto del Capitán]. You don't need to give a reason: from then on, we won't write to you.

At the same address you can ask what data we have about your business, and ask us to correct it or delete it.

We don't sell or share your data

We don't sell, rent or share your business data with other companies. Only we use it, and only for what this notice says.
