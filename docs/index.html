How to use them

  • 1. Pick one agent. One per chat. If you want the Contramaestre for your work and Quintero to sell, use two chats or two projects.
  • 2. Paste it at the start. As the first message of a new chat, or in the instructions of a Project (Claude, ChatGPT) or a Gem (Gemini).
  • 3. Fill in your context. At the end of each prompt there’s a block with your details: project, line of work, goal. That’s what makes it yours.
