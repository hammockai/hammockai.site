What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to computer systems designed to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence. These include learning from experience, understanding natural language, solving problems, and recognizing patterns.

Modern AI applications leverage machine learning algorithms that improve over time as they're exposed to more data. This enables systems to make predictions, automate processes, and provide insights that would be difficult or impossible to achieve manually.
