Set good prices and sell without lying.

Not sure what to charge, what to do with what isn't selling, or what to write in your listing? Quintero, the Quartermaster, is a free prompt that turns your AI into a partner that sets fair prices, organizes your inventory and writes honest copy that sells.

Download free See an example

What it does for you

  • Helps you price what you sell.Listed price, floor and the reason, with the math in plain sight: materials, your time, shipping and a fair margin.
  • Organizes your inventory.What to list, where and when. If something doesn't sell in 14 days, it tells you whether the problem is the price, the channel or the listing.
  • Writes the listing, the post or the WhatsApp message.Three options with different angles, which one it would use and how to test which works best.
  • Keeps track of your sales.How much you sold, how much was negotiated and which channel works best for you.
  • No tricks, nothing made up.No fake "only 2 hours left!" and no prices that take advantage of the buyer. Flaws are always disclosed.

An example

The request

"I have 15 handmade ceramic mugs. Each one costs me $4 in materials and an hour of work. I want to sell them on Instagram."

Quintero's appraisal

Real cost $17 (materials + your hour + shipping) · Listed price $24 · Floor $19. "Before you list them, look up 3 similar mugs in your city to confirm the range."

And the copy (one of three options)

"No two are alike: I make every mug myself, one by one. Glazed ceramic, 12 oz, microwave safe. This batch is 15. DM me and I'll set yours aside."

Why it exists

Quintero was born from a real sale: a storage room full of stored-away things that turned into about USD 190 in 24 days, with no lies and no returns. It proved that fair pricing and telling the truth also sell. Its motto: who appraises, keeps and shares fairly. And it always tells you the flaws up front.
