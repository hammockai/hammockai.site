Your site, step by step

This is what happens after you request your site and accept the quote. Tap each step to see what you do and what your package includes.

  1. 1
    You send your information You send us your texts, photos and details. You

    You do

    • Send your texts, your photos (with permission to use them) and your logo, if you have one.
    • Confirm your hours, address and contact details in writing.
    • Start thinking about your domain: your site’s web address, like yourbusiness.com or yourbusiness.cl.

    Your package includes

    • The quote in writing: pages, sections and rounds of changes.
    • Your timeline starts here: one page, 10 business days; simple website, 20; custom, 30.
    • Nothing made up: whatever is missing is marked, and we ask you.
  2. 2
    The crew builds The agents put your site together in small steps. The crew

    You do

    • Nothing. If something isn’t clear, we ask you.

    Your package includes

    • Design with your colors and logo, phone first.
    • The extras you asked for (form, booking or catalog), already connected.
    • Everything is checked before delivery: security, accessibility and nothing made up.
    • No trackers.
  3. 3
    You review it You see your site and tell us what to change. YouThe developer

    You do

    • You review your site with the developer, on a video call or with phone and computer screenshots.
    • Send all your changes in one message, as a list: where it is and what it should say (a screenshot helps). Each list counts as one round.

    Your package includes

    • One page: 1 round
    • Simple website: 2
    • Custom: 3
    • Anything outside your package is quoted separately.
    • We show you your site before it’s published.
  4. 4
    Final adjustments We apply your changes and you give the OK. The crewYou

    You do

    • Check the changes and approve them in writing. Without your OK, nothing goes live.

    Your package includes

    • Your changes applied, plus a last check: phone, accessibility and security.
    • Your files in a compressed folder (.zip): they’re yours.
    • A short guide, in plain words, to look after your site and ask for changes.
  5. 5
    Going live Your site goes online, in your name. YouThe developer

    You do

    • Buy your domain, in your name and with your email. It’s paid separately (below).
    • Create your free Cloudflare account with your email, turn on two-step security (a code on your phone) and save your recovery codes.
    • Do the clicks with the developer on a video call, or invite them to your account and remove them when done. You never share your password.

    Your package includes

    • Your site published, with the padlock (https).
    • A check together: it opens well on your phone, and a mistyped address shows a notice page.
    • Your site stays online even if you don’t sign up for maintenance.

    How free hosting works

    1. Your filesthe .zip we hand you
    2. Your Cloudflare accountfree plan, in your name
    3. Your domainyour site’s web address
    4. People looking for yousee your site, with the padlock

    Hosting is the place on the internet where your site lives, like the shop where you serve customers. We use Cloudflare Pages, which has a free plan. The account is yours, not ours: if one day you want to change provider or designer, you take your files and carry on.

    The domain is separate: it’s your site’s name. You pay for it every year to whoever sells it to you, not to us.
