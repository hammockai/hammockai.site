A hammock isn’t laziness: it’s sovereignty. We use AI to give you your time back, not to replace people.

Questions

How long does it take?

One page, 8 business days; simple website, 15; custom, 23 to 30. The count starts when you send your texts and photos.

Who owns the site?

You do: files, domain and content, with documentation in plain language.

What isn’t included?

A logo with a drawn symbol, a photo shoot, an online store with payments, a blog, advertising or another language.

Does the AI do it all on its own?

No. The agents do the heavy lifting in small steps and the founder reviews it with you before it's delivered.
