Included in every project

No add-ons for the basics. Every site we deliver comes with the same foundations.

  • Mobile-first, hand-written code. HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No heavy frameworks, nothing you can't read.
  • SEO foundations. Semantic markup, titles and descriptions, social previews, fast loading.
  • Accessibility basics. Readable type, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support.
  • No analytics or trackers by default. Your visitors aren't followed around the web from your site.
  • Full handover. Source files, domain in your name, and plain-language documentation.

Privacy by default

Low or zero telemetry, on purpose

Most small-business sites ship with analytics, pixels and embeds they don't need. Each one adds weight, third-party cookies and another company watching your visitors. We leave them out unless you have a clear reason to add them.

  • Faster pages

    Fewer scripts means faster loads, especially on mobile data. Speed helps both your visitors and your search ranking.

  • More trust

    People notice when a site respects them. No trackers means no one profiling your customers through your own website.

  • Less to manage

    No tracking cookies means fewer consent banners, fewer policies to keep updated and fewer third parties to depend on.

Need numbers? We'll propose privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics and explain the trade-off before adding anything. Your data, your rules, your freedom.

How we work

One developer. An AI crew. A human at the wheel.

Hammock AI is a one-person studio backed by three AI agents with clearly defined roles. The developer, the Captain, sets the goal and has the final say. Nothing ships without human review.

  • CAPTAIN · HUMAN

    The Developer

    Defines the goal and the boundaries, makes every decision and holds the final veto.

  • FIRST MATE · AI

    Contramaestre

    Challenges assumptions, maps the route and keeps the rhythm. Acts as a senior full-stack reviewer and mentor.

  • NAVIGATOR · AI

    Prompt Engineer

    Turns ideas into precise instructions for AI tools and keeps every message, prompt and line of copy clear.

  • COMPASS · AI

    Guardian

    Reviews the work against the Six Pillars before deployment, names hidden risks and proposes practical fixes.

The workflow

  1. The Captain defines the goal and the boundary.
  2. The Contramaestre checks for flaws and maps the route.
  3. The Prompt Engineer crafts a precise instruction.
  4. An AI coding tool executes it in small, verifiable chunks.
  5. The Guardian reviews the result against the Six Pillars.
  6. The Captain decides, and the crew keeps building.

We also tested a separate engineering agent and retired it: direct collaboration between the developer, the Contramaestre and focused coding tools proved faster and better aligned.

Principles

The Six Pillars

Every decision, prompt and line of code is filtered through six principles. Here's what they mean for you as a client.

  • 01

    Balanced Sea

    Deals that benefit both sides. Fair prices, mutual benefit over individual advantage, no zero-sum thinking.

  • 02

    The Booger Rule

    Be the friend who points out the booger and hands a tissue. If something won't work, we say so early, and bring the fix.

  • 03

    Challenge Assumptions

    We make assumptions explicit and verify them before building on top of them.

  • 04

    Accessibility First

    Plain-language docs and interfaces that don't intimidate. We explain the how and the why.

  • 05

    Open-Source Ethos

    Knowledge should be free and improvable. We publish our prompts and workflows, and favor auditable solutions.

  • 06

    Sovereign Ship

    Your data, your rules, your freedom. You own your work and can migrate anytime without losing what matters.

About

Why “Hammock”?

A hammock isn't about laziness; it's about sovereignty. It's the symbol of working smart enough to reclaim your time.

Hammock AI exists to help independent professionals and small teams build that same freedom, using AI not to replace people, but to give them their time back. A self-taught studio from Santiago & Pichilemu, Chile.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How much does a website cost?

It depends on scope. Send the brief and you'll get a clear, fair quote before any work begins. The time our AI-assisted workflow saves comes off your price.

Who owns the website?

You do. The source files, the domain and the content are yours, and we hand everything over with plain-language documentation.

Can I move my site to another provider later?

Yes. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript run on almost any hosting. No proprietary builder, no lock-in.

Will my site have analytics?

Not by default. If you need visitor numbers, we'll propose privacy-friendly, cookieless options and explain the trade-off before adding anything.

Is AI building my site unsupervised?

No. AI tools do the heavy lifting in small, verifiable steps, and a human reviews every change before it goes live.

Do you work in Spanish?

Yes, in Spanish and English. The brief is available in both languages, and your site can be in Spanish, English or both.
