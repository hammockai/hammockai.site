    Rest of the world: USD 450Chile: From CLP 150,000

    10 business days

    A clear message and a clear way to reach you.

    • Template in your colors and logo
    • Up to 10 sections and 8 photos
    • WhatsApp button with your number
    • A form to your email and spreadsheet, included to get you started
    • 1 round of changes

    One page gathers everything in one place. If you’d rather give each service its own page, so each one can show up in searches for that service, that comes with the Simple website.

    Technical details
    • One page on one of the portfolio templates, adapted with your colors (variables in :root) and your logo. Contact without a server: wa.me links with the message written, tel: and mailto:.
    • You send the texts and photos with a guide form; we organize and polish them. No writing from scratch.
    • Inquiries arrive by WhatsApp, phone, email or the form (included): what is stored, where, and in whose name is in the form’s technical details, further down. The site’s CSP opens only to the exact Google script URL (form-action and connect-src).
    • Everything shared (hosting, security, accessibility, search, images) is under “What’s inside”.
    • Search: a single page has one title, one address and one topic, so it can match fewer searches. In the Simple website each page gets its own title, address and topic (for example, one per service). Nobody can promise a position on Google: it also depends on your Google listing, reviews and competition.

  • Custom website

    Rest of the world: From USD 2,500Chile: From CLP 690,000

    30 business days

    Your own design, no template, built from your brand. Final price once we know your project.

    • A diagnosis: what works today, what’s missing and what to say first (for your business and for your current site, if you have one)
    • A style guide and a mockup you approve before we build
    • Your own design, from your brand, no template
    • Up to 8 pages, with texts written with you in a 45-minute interview
    • A form to your email and spreadsheet, plus booking or a catalog with “Order on WhatsApp” (your choice)
    • 3 rounds of changes and a 30-minute guided handover by video call
    Technical details
    • Process: (1) diagnosis: we read your current site as a visitor (what works, contact from a phone, trust, local search, language) and mark what is verified and what isn’t; (2) style guide: what you offer and to whom, the main action, what is fixed (name, logo, your texts) and what is free (palette, fonts, layout), language and what’s missing; (3) an HTML mockup for you to approve; (4) only then do we build. On the same technical base as “What’s inside”: no frameworks, strict CSP, automated and manual review.
    • Own design: no template; colors and fonts come from your brand. Fonts under an open license (OFL) are hosted on the site itself, never from Google Fonts.
    • Up to 8 pages, texts written with you, 3 rounds of changes (one message with all your changes in a list counts as one round).
    • Automations included: the form (→ email + spreadsheet) and one of these two: booking with reminder or a catalog with “Order on WhatsApp”. Service, account, data and third-party costs are in each add-on’s technical details. A catalog with more than 20 products with photos adds an extra tier.
    • Interview and handover: a 45-minute interview to write the texts with you and, at the end, a 30-minute guided handover by video call.
